# R001 – Business Problem

## Background

Modern organizations generate workforce data from multiple systems including recruitment, HRIS, payroll, learning, performance, attendance, and engagement platforms.

Despite having large volumes of employee data, many organizations struggle to convert this information into actionable insights.

As a result, HR leaders often rely on reactive reporting instead of proactive workforce planning.

---

# Current Challenges

## Recruitment

- Long hiring cycles
- High cost per hire
- Low sourcing efficiency

---

## Workforce Planning

- Limited visibility into workforce capacity
- Poor demand forecasting
- Skill shortages

---

## Performance Management

- Inconsistent performance evaluation
- Limited performance insights

---

## Employee Engagement

- Low engagement visibility
- Limited survey analytics

---

## Learning

- Training effectiveness not measurable
- Skill gaps remain unidentified

---

## Rewards

- Pay equity concerns
- Compensation benchmarking challenges

---

## Retention

- High voluntary attrition
- Unknown turnover drivers
- Critical talent loss

---

# Business Impact

Poor workforce decisions result in:

- Increased recruitment costs
- Lower productivity
- Reduced employee satisfaction
- Higher turnover
- Inefficient workforce allocation
- Weak executive visibility

---

# Project Opportunity

Develop a centralized People Intelligence Platform that integrates workforce data into a scalable analytics environment, enabling strategic and data-driven HR decision-making.
