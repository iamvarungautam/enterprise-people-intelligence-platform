# A001 – Business Architecture

## Purpose

The Business Architecture defines how the Enterprise People Intelligence Platform supports strategic HR and business decision-making.

---

# Core Business Domains

The platform is organized into nine business domains:

1. Workforce Planning
2. Talent Acquisition
3. Performance Management
4. Rewards & Compensation
5. Learning & Development
6. Employee Engagement
7. Workforce Operations
8. Talent Retention
9. Executive Decision Intelligence

---

# Business Capability Model

```
Enterprise People Intelligence Platform
```
