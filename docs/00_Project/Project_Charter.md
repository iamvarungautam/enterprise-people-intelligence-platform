# Project Charter

## Project Name

Enterprise People Intelligence Platform

---

## Project Vision

To design and develop an enterprise-grade People Intelligence Platform that demonstrates modern HR analytics, data engineering, business intelligence, and AI capabilities using industry best practices.

---

## Problem Statement

Organizations generate large volumes of workforce data across recruitment, employee lifecycle, performance, rewards, learning, engagement, and retention.

However, this information is often siloed across multiple systems, making strategic workforce decision-making difficult.

This project addresses this challenge by building a centralized analytics platform that transforms raw HR data into actionable business insights.
